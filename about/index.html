About

Christian H. Moore

Christian H. Moore is a pastor, missionary and writer with Greater Grace World Outreach. For more than thirty years he has served in counselling, leadership development and cross-cultural ministry — among them five years planting a church in Kraków, Poland, and many years of mission work across Ukraine, Central Asia and Europe.

He and his wife Gosia now live in Sicily, where he is planting Chiesa EverGrace in Messina.

Being in The Way was his blog from 2008 to 2016: short reflections, sermon notes, prayers and stories from the mission field. Since 2025 he has written Manna for Today, short devotionals shared with the church in Sicily. All of it is collected here in the blog archive.

He is the author of Prayer on Purpose: The 30 Day Prayer Guide into His Presence.

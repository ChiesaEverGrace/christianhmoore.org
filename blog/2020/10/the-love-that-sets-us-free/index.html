Being in The Way

The Love That Sets Us Free

24 October 2020 · 2020 archive

"In this is love, not that we loved God, but that He loved us and sent His Son to be the propitiation for our sins." — 1 John 4:10 NKJV

Hudson Taylor said that if he had a thousand lives, he'd give them all for China.

Jim Elliot said he is no fool who gives what he cannot keep to gain what he cannot lose.

Where does a love like that come from?

Not from trying harder. It comes from being loved first.

"For the love of Christ compels us… that those who live should live no longer for themselves, but for Him who died for them and rose again" (2 Corinthians 5:14–15).

What Calvary's love does

God's love doesn't demand change. It produces it.

"I will give you a new heart… I will put My Spirit within you and cause you to walk in My statutes" (Ezekiel 36:26–27). He does the causing.

God's love doesn't just ask for a few rights here and there. It asks for the whole heart. "My son, give me your heart" (Proverbs 23:26).

And our faith needs that love to stay alive. Faith works through love (Galatians 5:6). Cut off from His love, faith starts to wither.

So don't try to fix people. Love them with Calvary's love.

Peter asked how many times he had to forgive. Seven? Jesus said seventy times seven (Matthew 18:21–22). It isn't a number. It's every time. We are debtors to forgive, because we've been forgiven.

God has one love language

There are a lot of great virtues in the Bible. The greatest is love (1 Corinthians 13:13).

Materialism is one-dimensional. It's all about acquiring. Love can't be bought. "If a man would give for love all the wealth of his house, it would be utterly despised" (Song of Solomon 8:7).

God has one love language. The cross.

"For God so loved the world that He gave…" (John 3:16).

Freedom is dangerous, but necessary

Freedom was one of the first gifts God gave man. "I have set before you life and death… therefore choose life" (Deuteronomy 30:19).

Why take that risk?

Because you can't force someone to love you. Without real freedom there can't be real love.

Freedom is the condition for love. Freedom is the condition for faith. Freedom is the condition for change and growth.

But left to ourselves, we didn't manage our freedom well. We chose the flesh.

So God moved first. "We love Him because He first loved us" (1 John 4:19).

Love takes risks

Read Hosea. God tells His prophet to go and love a wife who keeps running away. That's how God loves Israel. That's how He loves us.

It's a wild love. It takes risks. Anything less is cheap love.

When Israel left her first love, she went looking for something she could control. Something that gave a quick thrill on her own terms.

We do it too. Somewhere along the road we decide that the romance we hoped for with God isn't going to happen. So we settle. We find substitutes. Things that are safer. Less painful. Less wild.

But indulgence never satisfies for long. Familiarity breeds contempt, and we go looking for the next thing.

Oswald Chambers said there is only One who can satisfy the last aching abyss of the human heart, and that is the Lord Jesus Christ.

He already moved toward you. At the cross.

What substitute have you settled for?

Being in The Way

Seven Truths About the Body of Christ

4 July 2020 · 2020 archive

"And He put all things under His feet, and gave Him to be head over all things to the church, which is His body, the fullness of Him who fills all in all." — Ephesians 1:22–23 NKJV

We tend to think of our faith as a private thing. Me and God.

But every believer is part of something bigger. A Body. And every Person of the Trinity is involved in it.

If we only understood what it means that we are in Christ, in a Body, with authority on this earth.

1. Authority

The Father put Christ as Head over everything, for the church.

That's why the armor of God in Ephesians 6 isn't written to a lone soldier. It's written to the Body. We share in His victory over the enemy together.

2. Sustained

"By one Spirit we were all baptized into one body—whether Jews or Greeks, whether slaves or free—and have all been made to drink into one Spirit" (1 Corinthians 12:13).

The Holy Spirit forms the Body. And the Holy Spirit keeps it alive.

3. Healing

Christ is "the Savior of the body" (Ephesians 5:23).

The Body is where healing happens. Where we grow up into Him, "speaking the truth in love," every joint supplying something (Ephesians 4:15–16).

You weren't meant to heal alone.

4. Reconciliation

Jew and Gentile. Every race. Every background.

He reconciled "both to God in one body through the cross, thereby putting to death the enmity" (Ephesians 2:16).

The walls that divide the world have no place in the Body. The cross already killed them.

5. Gifted

"We, being many, are one body in Christ, and individually members of one another" (Romans 12:5).

The Body is where we find our gifts. You won't discover what God put in you sitting on the sidelines.

6. A place of truth

He gave apostles, prophets, evangelists, pastors and teachers "for the equipping of the saints for the work of ministry, for the edifying of the body of Christ" (Ephesians 4:11–12).

The Body is built up as the Word is communicated. That's how we grow into "the measure of the stature of the fullness of Christ."

7. Sacred

The Body is made of sinners saved by grace. We know that. We see each other's flaws.

But "from now on, we regard no one according to the flesh" (2 Corinthians 5:16).

How do we do that practically?

We put the cross between us. We take offenses to the cross. And if something needs to be said, we go to the person alone, in private (Matthew 18:15).

The Body is sacred because it's His.

Where is your place in it?

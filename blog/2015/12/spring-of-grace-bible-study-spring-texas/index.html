Being in The Way…

Spring of Grace Bible Study - Spring Texas

December 2015 · 2015 archive


Are you in the Houston area? Want to connect with Greater Grace? 
We will be there & want to meet you Saturday 6.30p on January 23rd at Holiday Inn Express & Suites-North 
We will speak on "How the Grace of God Empowers" - we will discuss how God's Grace seeks to empower is in our lives and desires to take us beyond anything we could ever qualify for or deserve. How Grace gives us the capacity to overcome life obstacles and gives us a Divine Purpose, to be changed and to be an instrument in seeing the lives of others changed as well. God's Grace does not demand change, it produces change. 
What to Expect:
> The meeting will start on time with prayer and some simple worship.
> The message will be about 25 minutes long with a time for Q&A after.
> There will be refreshments served and a children's program is planned as well.

Being in The Way

The Bread That Satisfies

17 September 2022 · 2022 archive

"The LORD is my shepherd; I shall not want." — Psalm 23:1 NKJV

I shall not want.

Do we believe that?

"The young lions lack and suffer hunger; but those who seek the LORD shall not lack any good thing" (Psalm 34:10).

Jesus said, "Do not labor for the food which perishes, but for the food which endures to everlasting life" (John 6:27).

In His day, most people couldn't afford meat. Bread was what kept them on their feet. And in the wilderness, God sent manna every morning. It gave strength. And it was sweet.

That's satisfaction. Power, and pleasure.

The search

When we're young, we look for things ordinary life can't give. An experience. A person. A dream.

Sooner or later life shows us those things don't satisfy. And we find out we have an appetite deeper than anything material. A spiritual hunger.

We think, I'll be different. I'll find it. But nobody has ever been different enough to be satisfied.

We live by a lie. If I just had this, I'd be happy. Then we get it, and we're still us.

We don't realize how deep the heart goes. Nothing in this world is big enough to fill it.

The disappointment

So what do we do when we can't find it?

Some of us keep searching. It must be out there.

Some of us get angry. We were denied what we deserved.

Some get driven. A better spouse, a better job, a better house.

Some fall into despair. Something must be wrong with me.

Some become disillusioned. It doesn't exist. And the heart gets hard.

And some detach. Don't love anything too much.

C.S. Lewis said that if I find in myself a desire that nothing in this world can satisfy, the most probable explanation is that I was made for another world.

The real problem

Augustine said our problem is disordered loves. What makes us who we are isn't so much what we believe or do. It's what we love most.

If I love my career more than my family, I'll destroy my family.

We were made by a God who is relational by nature, Father, Son and Spirit. Made in His image. Made to love Him. That's where satisfaction is.

Live long enough and your heart will be broken. You will bury people you love.

If your love is fixed on anything other than God, you will be disappointed.

The Bread of Life

Jesus didn't say, "I'll give you bread."

He said, "I am the bread of life. He who comes to Me shall never hunger" (John 6:35).

Think about bread. It goes stale. It dies. And it has to be broken to be eaten.

Why would the immortal God taste death? Why would He let Himself be broken?

Love.

He is the bread that strengthens us. He is the bread that satisfies.

When we're shepherded by our own desires, we're always hungry again. When we're shepherded by the love of the Shepherd, we finally don't want.

Who is shepherding you today?

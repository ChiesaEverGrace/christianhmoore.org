Being in The Way

Only Say the Word

1 May 2022 · 2022 archive

"But say the word, and my servant will be healed." — Luke 7:7 NKJV

A Roman centurion had a servant who was dying.

He sent word to Jesus. And as Jesus got close to the house, the centurion sent friends with a message.

Lord, don't trouble Yourself. I'm not worthy for You to come under my roof. Just say the word.

I'm a man under authority. I tell one soldier "Go," and he goes. I tell another "Come," and he comes.

Jesus marveled. "I have not found such great faith, not even in Israel!" (Luke 7:9).

And when they got back to the house, the servant was well.

No touch. No visit. Just a word.

Faith comes by hearing

"So then faith comes by hearing, and hearing by the word of God" (Romans 10:17).

The word there is rhēma. A spoken word.

Not by seeing. By hearing.

It's interesting that one of the Ten Commandments forbids making images of God for worship. Why? If a picture helps you pray, what's the harm?

Here's the harm. An image hands you a ready-made version of God. Somebody else's idea of Him, already digested. The Word does something different. The Spirit takes it and opens your mind and heart to who He really is.

Words make you see

Some years ago the New York Times reported on research into reading to small children. Kids who were read to had far more activity in the parts of the brain that form pictures, even though they weren't looking at any.

A video does the imagining for you. A story makes you do it yourself.

That's how faith works. God speaks, and the heart begins to see what the eyes can't.

"Faith is the substance of things hoped for, the evidence of things not seen" (Hebrews 11:1).

"By faith we understand that the worlds were framed by the word of God, so that the things which are seen were not made of things which are visible" (11:3).

The whole world came from a word.

Living and powerful

"For the word of God is living and powerful, and sharper than any two-edged sword, piercing even to the division of soul and spirit" (Hebrews 4:12).

It does what images can't. It goes in deep. It reaches the place where we decide what we love.

The centurion understood authority. When the right person speaks, it happens.

So what is He saying to you today?

Only say the word, Lord.

Being in The Way

Fear Not, Little Flock

2018 · 2018 archive

"Fear not, little flock; for it is your Father's good pleasure to give you the kingdom." — Luke 12:32 KJV

There's fear. And there's anxiety.
We use the words like they're the same thing. They aren't.

Fear has something I can point to. That. Something I love is threatened. Watch out. Protect it.

Anxiety is harder. I can't point to anything. It isn't what I love that feels threatened. It's who I am.
I'm frail. I'm not enough. Something is wrong with me.

We know this one.

Where it started

In the garden, "they were both naked, the man and his wife, and were not ashamed" (Genesis 2:25).

Then sin came in. And the first thing Adam says to God is, "I heard Your voice in the garden, and I was afraid because I was naked; and I hid myself" (Genesis 3:10).

Afraid. Naked. Hid.

Fear and shame showed up together, and they've been travelling together ever since.

Here's the thing.
The fear of failure is really the fear of shame.

Guilt says, I did something wrong.
Shame says, There is something wrong with me.

And when we feel shame, we look for covering. Fig leaves. Something to hide behind.

Think about that. The very garden Adam was made to rule over in God's authority, he's now hiding in.

We still do it. We control. We perform. We manage how people see us. We try to protect ourselves from ever being exposed again.

Real assets cannot be lost

When that deep anxiety comes, when you feel the frailty of everything and the fear of failing, ask yourself a very simple question.

What are my real assets? The ones that can't be taken?

  1. His relationship with you. "I will never leave you nor forsake you." So we may boldly say: "The Lord is my helper; I will not fear." (Hebrews 13:5–6)
  2. His love for you. "I have loved you with an everlasting love" (Jeremiah 31:3). "In all these things we are more than conquerors through Him who loved us" (Romans 8:37).
  3. Your identity in Jesus. "If anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation; old things have passed away; behold, all things have become new" (2 Corinthians 5:17).
  4. Your salvation. "By grace you have been saved through faith… it is the gift of God, not of works" (Ephesians 2:8–9).
  5. His grace. Enough for every weakness (2 Corinthians 12:9).

None of these depend on how well you did today.
None of them can be lost.

You are the object of intense love and value in the Finished Work.

So what do we do?

God calls us to turn away from the false safety of controlling our own protection from shame.
Stop sewing fig leaves.
Stop trying to control the garden.

And rely entirely on Him.

He isn't holding the kingdom back from you, waiting to see if you'll measure up.
It's His good pleasure to give it.

Fear not, little flock.

What fig leaf are you holding today?

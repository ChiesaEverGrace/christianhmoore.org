Being in The Way

Moving to Philly

January 2013 · 2013 archive



Revelation 3:7-8 "And to the angel of the church in Philadelphia write; These things saith he that is holy, he that is true, he that hath the key of David, he that openeth, and no man shutteth; and shutteth, and no man openeth; I know thy works: behold, I have set before thee an open door, and no man can shut it" 
As you know or may have heard, we are moving to Philadelphia to focus on the mission we have in that region. This process has been a slow, step by step walk of faith in God's adventure for us. We are thrilled and humbled to think God has chosen us to do this for His Name's sake. We are missionaries in the United States. Now we are ministering in 3 locations in Philly and with a growing local team and staff in Philly we sense that now is an exciting point in the ministry to this city. Our life there will be engrossed in investment in our new friends in Philly and reaching out to the city with pastor Tony, Ann Marie Pallotta and our resident team there. The advantage of the geo-location is that Philly is only 1.5 hrs away and we can be in Baltimore often.

Though this is a bold-new step for Gosia and I we are excited and we're a team. We know God is with us and that we have an awesome church, friends and family in Baltimore sending us off. Finally, over the next couple of weeks if you'd like to help out give us a call. We welcome your prayer and support.
